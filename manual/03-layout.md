# Layout

Layout is intentionally simple. Drag objects to move them, drag their handles to resize them, and use the alignment controls under **Props** when several objects are selected.

With nothing selected, **Props → Layout** offers three starting points: **Freeform**, **Title + body**, and **Title slide**. These only place text boxes; the theme controls how they look.

### Links in text

While editing text, select some words and press **Cmd+K** (**Ctrl+K** on Windows and Linux), or click **Link…** under **Props → Typography**. Type an address and press Return. With the cursor inside a link, **Cmd/Ctrl+K** shows its address to change, and **Remove link** turns it back into plain text. With nothing selected it inserts the address itself as a link.

Pasting an address over selected words also links them, and an address you type becomes a link when you finish it with a space or Return. Links open in a new browser tab, while editing and while presenting.
